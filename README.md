# QuickBite Refund Process Improvement (BA Case Study)

> Fictional client. All thresholds, targets and figures are stated assumptions. The AS-IS process is a simplified model inferred from public reviews, not a real company's process.

## Problem
Food delivery customers report slow, inconsistent refunds, coupons offered instead of money, no status updates and hard-to-reach support. This case study defines requirements for a faster, more transparent refund process.

## My role
Business Analyst: research, process mapping, requirements, user stories and traceability.

## Approach
1. Read public Google Play reviews of two food delivery apps and grouped 20 complaint points into 6 themes.
2. Modelled the current (AS-IS) process and identified 6 pain points.
3. Designed an improved (TO-BE) process addressing each pain point.
4. Wrote 15 prioritized requirements, 8 business rules and 7 edge cases.
5. Wrote 11 user stories with acceptance criteria and mapped everything in a traceability matrix.
