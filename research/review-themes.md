# Review Themes: Refund Pain Points

**Method:** I read public Google Play Store reviews of Swiggy and Zomato and noted 20 complaint points in my own words (Swiggy 11, Zomato 9). I grouped each point under one main theme, so the counts add up to 20.

| # | Theme | What customers reported | Swiggy | Zomato | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missing, wrong or damaged items | Items missing from the order, wrong item received, food spilled because of poor packaging | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| 2 | Refund denied or replaced by coupons | No refund for an undelivered item, coupons offered instead of money, no resolution for missing items | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| 3 | Support hard to reach | No response from support, no way to contact support, chatbot that does not understand the problem | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| 4 | Payment and cancellation problems | Paid twice because of an app glitch, no proper way to cancel an accidental order, order cancelled without notice with a fee charged, payment failed but order still confirmed | 3 | 1 | 4 |
| 5 | Delivery delays | Food not delivered on time, slow delivery, combined deliveries causing delay | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| 6 | Fees and app issues (outside refund scope) | Login problems, extra fee for cash on delivery, GST charged separately | 1 | 2 | 3 |

## Key takeaways
- 13 of the 20 points (themes 1 to 4) relate directly to refund-triggering events or to how refunds and claims are handled.
- The most common pattern is that when an item is missing, wrong or damaged, customers get no refund or only coupons, and struggle to reach a person who can help.
- Late deliveries (theme 5) are a frequent trigger for claims. Theme 6 is out of scope for this project.
